# Aster Desktop Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Aster Desktop a respectful, safe, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive, specific technical feedback.
- Focusing discussion on the work, its documented requirements, and verifiable evidence.
- Acknowledging mistakes, correcting them promptly, and learning from the experience.
- Respecting privacy, confidentiality, and the project's security-reporting process.
- Prioritizing the health and safety of the community and the users of the software.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring anyone to reveal credentials, private conversations, vulnerability details, or other sensitive information.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, issue triage, or project infrastructure.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, meetings, and other collaboration channels. It also applies when someone officially represents Aster Desktop in a public space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Use GitHub's private reporting or moderation controls when they are available, or a private contact method published by the repository owner. Do not post personal information, private correspondence, credentials, or sensitive vulnerability details in a public issue.

If no private maintainer channel is visible, use GitHub's platform reporting tools. A security vulnerability must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct-reporting process.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report as soon as reasonably practical, review it impartially, and protect the reporter's privacy to the greatest extent possible. Anyone involved in a report must recuse themselves from deciding its outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate and proportionate, including:

1. **Correction**: a private explanation and request to correct minor unprofessional behavior.
2. **Warning**: a formal warning with conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction**: time-limited removal from project interaction or decision-making.
4. **Permanent restriction**: permanent removal from project spaces for sustained, severe, or repeated violations.

Retaliation against a reporter, witness, or participant in an enforcement process is itself a violation. Maintainers will document enforcement decisions privately and share only the minimum information needed to protect the community.
